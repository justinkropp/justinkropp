I am, depending on the day and who is asking, a product designer, a musician, a Graphic Designer, an identical twin, a motorcyclist, a photographer, a fledgling writer, a contrarian, and — a development I find increasingly congenial — an outsider. None of these labels, taken individually, does the full job. Taken together, however, they account for a reasonably accurate outline of whatever it is that one means when one says I.

I don't maintain an online portfolio, nor am I active on social media. My free time is spent reading, playing music, and exploring the world on two wheels.
